The Codex Cronenberg:

David Cronenberg is a director that keeps popping up on the periphery of movies that I watch. Everyone I know has a favorite Cronenberg, everyone seems to walk away from his movies with completely different ideas. So much of his work was making its way onto my watch list that I decided to actually just pull the trigger and watch every single one of his movies in a row. I have only seen two of his shorts and none of his TV documentaries but I can say now that I have watched all of his feature films. I also brought in other directors to fill out the list and really explore some of the themes in Body Cinema.

This is one of the most valuable cinematic experiences I have ever had, I was surprised at what I found in his works and just how thoroughly he developed some of the same ideas over and over in every subsequent film. I found a new favorite director in Shinya Tsukamoto. I’ve learned more about analysis of movies as an art form going through this list than I have in all the previous years watching movies combined. I hope that I’m able to convey any of this in the following write-up of the experience.

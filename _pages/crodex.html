The Codex Cronenberg:

David Cronenberg is a director that keeps popping up on the periphery of movies that I watch. Everyone I know has a favorite Cronenberg, everyone seems to walk away from his movies with completely different interpretations of his work and what it all means.

This is one of the most valuable cinematic experiences I have ever had, I was surprised at what I found in his works and just how thoroughly he developed some of the same ideas over and over in every film he makes.

There's almost 40 movies here and even doing one paragraph for each would drive me insane so I'm going to be pairing and tripling them up and discussing their overlaps more so than the movies individually.

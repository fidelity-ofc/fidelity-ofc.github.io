Your Material Sphere consists of the Reversed Nine of Stakes, the Reversed Queen of Pentacles, and the Queen of Scythes.

I am reading a creature who has faced a significant trial and has since come to fear opening themselves again lest they fall into another trial that will extract an even heavier price. Consequently, they find themselves in a constant state of precarity. The material world around them is fraught with potential loss of position, that they find themselves acting as the barrier between loved ones and failure or danger. What allows them to navigate these circumstances, however, is clear eyes that survey the world and find the correct path forward. The discerning and decisive gaze that both identifies the obstacle and plots the course around (or through) it.

Your Immaterial Sphere consists of the Six of Pentacles, the Knight of Pentacles, and the Knight of Chalices

This person is driven by a singularly benevolent purpose: the ability to grant the desires of others. They draw great inner strength from fulfilling these material needs for other people, people who are in need of aid. This drive is supplemented by the impulse to maintain a grounded outlook and follow a course of steady progress for the needy. That impulse can only be sustained by the power that is drawn from a very different source: emotional vulnerability. They need a place, a psychic location, where the knight can remove their armor and be seen, for someone to see them and to feel them in kind.

to bring all of this together,

The Knighted Queen described above is imbalanced. Both the Material and Immaterial Spheres carry a focus on the physical which leaves the Knight of Cups alone in their attempt to find that location of solace. The reversed cards reveal a position of self-imposed isolation, that despite providing for those they love, they maintain a distance that prevents the bonds that would satisfy the needs of the Knight of Cups. Perhaps it is a suppression of the self in order to better tend to the others or perhaps it is a fear that whatever trial previously endured will come once again. The Queen and the other knight simply act on what is before them, oblivious to the psychic imbalance that is occuring and still forging ahead, steady and forceful, as they believe that the Desire to Provide, the center of fulfillment, will always be best fueled this way. But eventually that Focus will lead to a burning out without the fuel derived from the Bond of the Knight of Cups.

As I perform this reading, I feel hopeful for the soul in question. It can feel wretchedly difficult to doff the armor and allow another to have access to their soft, vulnerable flesh but it is easier than it may seem. Many lives have been enriched by the subject and if they knew the need, they'd more than happily fulfill it. To connect, whether it is a meal shared, cries listened, tears dried, lips parted, or pillows rested. The path forward is easy, it is simply clouded by the fear of the 9 of Stakes and the Queen of Pentacles and their inversion. It won't be until the Knight of Cups is addressed that they will once again right themselves and clear the blockage. I must admit, I could not quite connect this figure to an identity. Whether it is you, the person requesting the reading, or if it is someone you are deeply concerned over. If someone came to mind as you read these words, perhaps you should help them unbuckle the armor they keep so tightly bound. Or if it is you, maybe you need to ask for help removing your own.

Neither could I pin down a temporal location, whether this is an image of who the subject once was or who they are on the path toward becoming. Regardless, take this reading for what it is: a simple tale told by the cards of someone hiding within a suit of armor. Whether it bears any truth is of no consequence to me as the reader. I only hope to given you something of value.

Your pearl was slow to melt, holding on to the thick waxy walls for a full minute before finally thinning enough to release the air and the feelings trapped within. It took a further minute and a half to completely meld with the rest of the wax from those who received readings before. Watch it dissolve and think about what you put inside it, see it escape from the shell as the object holding it joins in the solidarity of the seekers.

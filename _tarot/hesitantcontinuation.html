Your Material Sphere consists of the Four of Swords, The Moon, and the Seven of Swords.

This indicates to me that you are yourself a sort of resting space. That your role is that of sanctuary for something or someone. This is complicated by how difficult it is to read you as what you are. The safety you can provide often appears illusory, or is believed to be so, by someone. This seems to be a result of how you have developed a knack for acting alone, navigating situations on your more decisive intellect rather than this intuitive drive. The sharp blades of intellect cannot cut through the illusions cast by the moonlight, after all.

Your Immaterial Sphere consists of the Five of Wands, the Four of Pentacles, and the Three of Swords

Opposite the Four of Swords is the Five of Wands. This, as your focus, indicates that a sense of rivalry should enliven you. That clashing with others could bring you a feeling of motivation. The Four of Pentacles complicates this as it is indicating an impulse to withhold that feeling of competition. After all, if you were to actually cause harm to someone, it might push them out of the sanctuary you provide. It might cause damage that can't be repaired, the loss of something that cannot be replaced. And with that feeling, the domain of the Three of Swords comes in to complicate further. It is that feeling of potential loss that gives you power. Nothing is as invigorating or motivating as the presence of bare steel.

to bring all of this together,

It's important to note what cards are opposite each other here. The Four of Swords is facing the Five of Wands. A real blade held in time of rest and the blunt rod in time of conflict. Swords are also in the domain of the mind while wands are physical yet they are respectively in the Material and Immaterial realm. This spread, to me, is trying very hard to highlight that, currently, you need to exercise your mental faculties in lower stakes conflict so that you can provide a feeling of safety for someone or something that is seeking it. It is difficult to be open with someone if you don't know how they respond under stress and duress, you see.

That is part of what the Three of hearts is saying about this whole ordeal. You must be open to the experience of pain and loss if you are to fulfill this need. You must define the shape of your space so that those seeking solace in you know where they can stand. You draw the lines that they can find space within. You tell them where they cannot go so they can see the places where they can. Right now, as the Seven of Swords indicates, you are holding too many of them in the act of handling things in solitude. While it prevents any of the swords from piercing the heart, that very threat is what you need to move forward from your current position. That it's time to start defining the walls of your sanctuary, so that another may recognize them and choose whether they will stand within them.

I feel, to be quite honest, as though there is an element that I have missed as I look over this spread. It strikes me that the entirety of your Material Sphere is dominated by cards that are within the mental realm. That 2/3 of your Immaterial cards are in the physical realm. I'm starting to believe that perhaps the above reading should be tempered by this but I am unsure of how. Perhaps it is the case that too much of your world is currently only being expressed in your own mind. These thoughts should be dragged out into tangibility, maybe that is what The Moon has been saying this whole time: you cannot let your intuition remain as a whisper in the back of your mind. You need to engage in a difficult discussion more tangibly.

Your pearl was slow to start but once a single hole formed, it eagerly rushed into the enveloping heat of the wax pool and dissipated into the swirling stress of the concerns of those previously read. Watch it dissolve and think about what you put inside it, see it escape from the shell as the object holding it joins in the solidarity of the seekers.
